## 11. Turning the forecast into a decision

A dispatcher can look at only a few stations at a time. At each decision time (every hour for the 1 h horizon, every 3 h and every 6 h for the longer ones, so windows never overlap) every ranking picks its top K stations, and we count how many of them really had a big net flow (the same cut-offs as section 1: ±9+ bikes for 1 h, ±18+ bikes for 3 h, ±29+ bikes for 6 h). *Big net flow is not the same as an empty or full station*: net flow ignores trucks and the real stock (section 12 scores that on real station counts). K stands in for real limits such as truck capacity. Choices and the rule for "better" were fixed before the numbers were looked at (the aggregate backtest had been seen once already). All numbers carry 95% day-block bootstrap intervals (2,000 resamples of whole days). *Better* only when the interval of the paired difference is wholly above zero; otherwise it is a tie.

- **precision@K**: the share of the K picks that were big.
- **capture@K**: picks that were big, divided by the smaller of K and the number of big windows at that time (1.0 = a perfect ranking). Times with no big window are left out.
- **Usual suspects**: each station's typical |net| for that hour of the week, from 2022 and 2023 only. It shows how much of the skill is just 'downtown stations are always big at rush hour'.
- **Random**: the exact expected result of picking at random from the same candidates. Every ranking picks from the busiest 100 stations in service at that time (median 85), not from the whole network.

## 12. Will the station be empty or full?

Net flow is not stock. Here the forecast is scored on real station counts: past snapshots of BIXI's station feed, saved about every 15 minutes by a public archive (Max Halford's bike-sharing-history), for summer 2025. 91 of 100 stations match. **This is a retrospective test on past data, not a prospective one.** Level 2 never trained on 2025. Questions, baselines and the rule for "better" were fixed before any result was seen (plan/05-empty-full-probability.md).

- **Empty**: zero bikes while the station is renting. **Full**: zero free docks while it takes returns. Measured at the snapshot nearest the end of the window (within 10 minutes).
- **Headline population**: stations that are not already empty (or full) at the start.
- **Starting-count rate** (the baseline to beat): how often a station with that many bikes (or free docks) ended up empty (or full) after that many hours, at that hour of day, learned on May to July 2025. It uses the real count but no forecast.
- **Direct**: Level 2's forecast and its 80% band read as a normal distribution. **Calibrated**: the same, passed through a one-variable logistic recalibration fitted on May to July 2025.
- **Score**: Brier score, lower is better. **Skill** = share of the baseline's Brier score removed. Tested on August and September 2025, with 95% day-block intervals. *Better* only if the paired interval is wholly above zero.
